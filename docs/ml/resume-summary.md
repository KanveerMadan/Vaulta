# Resume summary

Generated by `ml/report.py`; every number is from `ml/artifacts/metrics.json`.

Built a transaction categoriser for Vaulta, an Indian personal-finance app, by knowledge distillation: two LLMs on Groq labelled 5018 stripped merchant names (a third broke ties) from a 130-subcategory taxonomy, and a 21,542-n-gram character TF-IDF + logistic-regression model (0.75 MB gzipped, identical in the browser and in Python) learned from 19,615 synthetic bank narrations. On merchants never seen in training it raised macro-F1 against the teacher labels from 7.0 to 44.7 and cut the share of transactions sent to manual review from 79.5% to 57.8%; on unseen local shops, from 86.5% to 31.2%, with 94.0% agreement where it files. Includes calibration (ECE 0.0429), per-user correction memory, reversible re-categorisation and opt-in retraining gated on frozen test sets; a 16 MB multilingual embedding alternative was evaluated and rejected.
